# Licensing

This repository uses different terms for different kinds of material. No
single license applies to every file. The rules below define the default for
original contributions to the Pristine brand system; an explicit notice next
to a file overrides the corresponding default. Contributors retain any
copyright they hold. This policy does not assign ownership or assert a
copyright year for Pristine material.

## Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International

Original Pristine documentation, design tokens, banners, illustrations, and
templates are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International Public License (`CC-BY-4.0`). This includes their editable
masters and ready-to-use exports when both are present.

The complete legal text is in
[`LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt`](LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt). The canonical license page
is <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>.

When redistributing or adapting this material, identify Pristine and the
credited creators, link to the license and source, preserve supplied copyright
and other required notices, and indicate changes. Fill the fields below from
the actual contribution record rather than inventing a holder or year:

> Pristine brand system, by `<credited creators>`.
> `<supplied copyright notice, if any>`. Licensed under CC BY 4.0:
> <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>. Source:
> <https://github.com/PristineEDA/brand>. Modified by `<name>`; changes:
> `<brief description>`.

For an unmodified copy, omit the modification sentence. Retain any notices of
previous modifications. Adapted upstream documentation additionally requires
the retroSoC attribution recorded below; Pristine attribution does not replace it.

## Adapted documentation

The repository guidance and policy documents adapt the CC-BY-4.0 documentation
in [retroSoC/brand at commit
92eef243de832fedbab4977e04a27aa410c2dc88](https://github.com/retroSoC/brand/tree/92eef243de832fedbab4977e04a27aa410c2dc88).
The upstream notice is preserved here:

> Copyright (c) 2026 retroSoC contributors.

The [third-party register](third-party/README.md#retrosoc-documentation)
identifies the affected files, source, license, and modifications. Preserve
that record when redistributing these adaptations. It attributes the upstream
text; it does not describe ownership of Pristine names or artwork. The adapted
documentation remains under CC-BY-4.0 and carries no upstream endorsement.

## MIT-licensed maintenance code

Original maintenance and asset-generation software under `scripts/`, when
added, is licensed under the MIT License. No maintenance software is currently
included.

[`LICENSES/MIT.txt`](LICENSES/MIT.txt) contains the complete standard MIT
template, with `<year>` and `<copyright holders>` left as explicit placeholders.
Before adding the first such software, replace those placeholders with verified
notices and record the applicable notices in or alongside each contributed
file. Preserve all required upstream notices for any reused code. Do not
substitute Pristine for another work's copyright owner or use the retroSoC
documentation notice as the copyright notice for new software.

## Names and official marks

The Pristine and PristineEDA names, wordmarks, logos, logo marks, and other
official brand marks are not licensed under CC-BY-4.0 or MIT. The limited
permissions for using them are defined in [`TRADEMARKS.md`](TRADEMARKS.md).
The policy text itself is documentation under CC-BY-4.0.

There is no blanket license for future content under `assets/source/icons/` or
`assets/export/icons/`. Each icon must carry or reference explicit licensing
and provenance information. An icon that functions as an official Pristine
mark is also governed by `TRADEMARKS.md`.

## Third-party material

Third-party material retains its upstream copyright and license. It is not
relicensed by this repository. Its creator, source, license, required notices,
and modifications must be recorded as described in
[`third-party/README.md`](third-party/README.md), with complete upstream
license text included when redistribution requires it.

The presence of a third-party work does not imply that its creator sponsors
or endorses Pristine or PristineEDA.
