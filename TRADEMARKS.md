# Pristine Trademark Policy

This policy governs use of the Pristine and PristineEDA names and official
brand marks. It is separate from the open licenses that apply to other
material in this repository.

Nothing in this document states or implies that a covered mark is registered
in a particular jurisdiction. No graphical marks are distributed in this
repository yet; this policy does not designate new artwork as an official mark.

## Covered marks

The covered marks are the Pristine and PristineEDA names, and wordmarks,
logos, logo marks, or other artwork expressly identified by the project as
official Pristine brand marks.

These marks are excluded from the CC-BY-4.0 and MIT licenses described in
[`LICENSE.md`](LICENSE.md). This policy grants only the limited permissions
written below, to the extent the project is entitled to grant them.

## Uses allowed without prior permission

You may, in a truthful and non-misleading manner:

- refer to Pristine or PristineEDA by name when discussing, reviewing,
  teaching, or reporting on the project;
- link to an official PristineEDA repository or project page; and
- display an unmodified official mark, when distributed by the project,
  solely to identify or link to the Pristine project.

These permissions apply only when the use does not imply sponsorship,
endorsement, affiliation, or official status and does not create confusion
about the source of a product, service, project, or publication.

## Uses requiring prior written permission

Prior written permission is required to:

- modify, redraw, recolor, animate, combine, or create a derivative of an
  official mark;
- use a covered mark in the name, logo, icon, domain name, social-media handle,
  or other identity of another product, project, organization, or event;
- place a covered mark on merchandise or other goods offered for sale or
  distribution;
- use a covered mark in advertising, sponsorship, fundraising, or other
  promotional material beyond the identification allowed above; or
- use a covered mark in any other way likely to imply endorsement, affiliation,
  or official status.

## Requesting permission

Open an issue at <https://github.com/PristineEDA/brand/issues> describing the
mark, the proposed use, the distribution channel, and whether the use is
commercial. Opening an issue does not grant permission. Permission exists
only after a PristineEDA maintainer authorized to approve the use gives
explicit written approval.

This policy does not restrict uses that applicable law permits without the
project's authorization.
